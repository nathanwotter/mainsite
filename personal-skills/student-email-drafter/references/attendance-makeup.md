# Attendance Makeup Reply

Use this policy for attendance makeup across every class Nathan teaches. A particular class meeting may require customization when it included an activity that cannot be replaced by the standard work.

## Policy

- Completing the makeup is optional.
- The student has one week after returning to complete it.
- For each missed class period, the student watches the lecture recording on Moodle and takes notes on the material covered.
- For each missed class period, the student records a roughly five-minute video response addressing all three prompts:
  1. What did the class cover? Summarize the major concepts in the student's own words, not as an AI-generated description or disconnected bullet-point snippets.
  2. How would the student respond to the questions asked or activities completed in class? The response should apply the material as the student would have done in class.
  3. What from the class was confusing, challenging, or could have been explained better? Include at least one item.
- The student may upload the video to Google Drive and share the link, or provide a private YouTube link.
- Invite questions.

## Adaptation rules

- Keep the requirements substantively intact, but write them naturally rather than copying a rigid form.
- State `for each missed class period` wherever needed to prevent the student from interpreting one video as covering multiple absences.
- If the message provides return timing, express the deadline as one week after that return. If return timing is unclear, say `within one week after you return` rather than inventing a calendar date.
- Acknowledge the stated reason for the absence briefly and appropriately. Never infer an unstated illness, emergency, or personal circumstance.
- If the thread identifies a class-specific activity that needs an alternate task, use only details Nathan has already provided. Otherwise flag the thread for customization instead of drafting unsupported requirements.

## Reference shape

The reply should normally contain:

1. A personalized greeting and one-sentence acknowledgment.
2. A short explanation that the following work allows the student to stay on track and make up the absence.
3. The Moodle recording and note-taking requirement.
4. A numbered list of the three video prompts.
5. Google Drive or private YouTube submission options.
6. The one-week-after-return deadline, framed as optional makeup.
7. An invitation for questions, a time-appropriate closing wish, and Nathan's current signature.
