# Course guest email patterns

Use these patterns for routine semester outreach without querying Gmail. Replace every bracketed field with current, supported information. Get dates, times, topics, and locations from the current course schedule or the user's request; never carry example dates into a new semester.

## Shared voice

- Open with `Hi, [First name]!` or `Hey [First name]!` for a familiar contact.
- For a returning collaborator, thank them and mention the specific prior contribution before asking again.
- Use short conversational paragraphs, contractions, and natural enthusiasm.
- Make one clear request with an exact weekday, date, time window, and expected role.
- Acknowledge obvious conflicts or workload when relevant, without overexplaining.
- Close with `Thanks a bunch for considering it` or `Thanks again`, followed by `Nathan`.
- Let Gmail apply the established university signature when available.

## PRT 152 — individual or returning guest

[Returning: Thanks so much for visiting my PRT 152 class [last term]. It was super-helpful for students to learn about [topic] and your career path/work.] [New: I teach PRT 152, Introduction to Parks, Recreation, Tourism and Event Management, and would love to invite you to visit the class to share about [topic/work].]

Would you be game to visit [again]? I think your perspective would fit especially well with our class on [weekday, month day] from [start time–end time]. [Add one sentence connecting the day's activity or topic to the guest.]

Nathan
